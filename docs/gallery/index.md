# Gallery

Projects built with Charming, from tools and interactive pieces to generative art. For smaller pieces with their code, see the [examples](/examples/).

## Generative Art

Art generated from text: letters packed into glyphs, and names grown into trees.

### APack

A canvas-like writing surface for packing Latin text into glyphs, for posters, names and clocks. [Open APack](https://apack.bairui.dev/)

[![APack](/gallery/apack.webp)](https://apack.bairui.dev/)

### Name to Tree

Type your name and it grows into a tree of its own, generated from the character codes of its letters. [Open Name to Tree](https://tree.bairui.dev/)

[![Name to Tree](/gallery/name-to-tree.webp)](https://tree.bairui.dev/)

## Pretext

Text layout with [Charming Pretext](/pretext).

### U.S. Map Paper

An interactive U.S. map whose states are filled with notes about them, made with Charming Pretext. [Open U.S. Map Paper](https://pretext.charmingjs.org/us-map-paper/)

[![U.S. Map Paper](/gallery/us-map-paper.webp)](https://pretext.charmingjs.org/us-map-paper/)

### Yin Yang

An animated Yin Yang symbol filled with descriptions of itself, made with Charming Pretext. [Open Yin Yang](https://pretext.charmingjs.org/yin-yang/)

[![Yin Yang](/gallery/yin-yang.webp)](https://pretext.charmingjs.org/yin-yang/)

### Air Text

A screen of text that parts around your body, seen through the webcam, made with Charming Pretext. [Open Air Text](https://pretext.charmingjs.org/air-text/)

[![Air Text](/gallery/air-text.webp)](https://pretext.charmingjs.org/air-text/)

## Music

Audiovisual pieces with [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/).

### Interactive Composition: Eye

An interactive composition: move the circle in the middle to mix the tracks of a song, drawn as an eye of volume bars and waveforms. [Open Interactive Composition: Eye](https://music.bairui.dev/bar)

[![Interactive Composition: Eye](/gallery/eye.webp)](https://music.bairui.dev/bar)

### AV Instrument: Streamgraph

An audiovisual instrument: play sounds with the keyboard and watch their waveforms pile up into a streamgraph. [Open AV Instrument: Streamgraph](https://music.bairui.dev/area)

[![AV Instrument: Streamgraph](/gallery/av-instrument.webp)](https://music.bairui.dev/area)

## Image

Works made from an image. Upload your own.

### Pixel Poetry

An image turned into a poem: packed circles take its colors and grow characters from its brightness, which are read out as verses. [Open Pixel Poetry](/gallery/pixel-poetry)

[![Pixel Poetry](/gallery/pixel-poetry.webp)](/gallery/pixel-poetry)

### Islamic Avatar

A portrait as a mosaic inspired by Islamic art, made of polygons whose sides and rotations follow noise. [Open Islamic Avatar](/gallery/islamic-avatar)

[![Islamic Avatar](/gallery/islamic-avatar.webp)](/gallery/islamic-avatar)

### Art Gallery Painter

A painter that recreates an image with brush strokes, getting finer as it goes. [Open Art Gallery Painter](/gallery/art-gallery-painter)

[![Art Gallery Painter](/gallery/art-gallery-painter.webp)](/gallery/art-gallery-painter)
